 END OF MISSION:
  The Dawn mission was completed when scheduled communication with the DSN did
  not occur on Oct 31 and Nov 01 of 2018.

 MISSION DESCRIPTION:
 Dawn's goal is to characterize the conditions and processes of the solar 
 system's earliest epoch by investigating in detail Ceres and Vesta, two of 
 the largest protoplanets remaining intact since formation.

  Vesta, cruise to Ceres, and spiral to a low altitude orbit at Ceres.
