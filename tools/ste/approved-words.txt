# Approved words of the Apsis project for the check with tools/ste/ste-lint.mjs.
#
# THIS IS A PROJECT LIST. IT IS NOT THE ASD-STE100 DICTIONARY.
# The official ASD-STE100 dictionary was not available. The author of this list made it from general knowledge
# of Simplified Technical English. No person compared the list with the official dictionary.
# The list has only the general words that the texts of the product and the documents in docs/ use, and the
# words that not-approved.json gives as alternatives. The option --unused of the tool shows the entries that no
# text uses. A new word is a decision: add it here only if you think that ASD-STE100 approves it, with its part
# of speech.
#
# Format of a line:   word   parts of speech   other forms
#   Parts of speech: n (noun), v (verb), adj, adv, prep, conj, pron, art. STE approves a word only in the parts
#   of speech of its dictionary entry. Example: "check" is a noun only, "close" is a verb only.
#   Other forms: for a verb "third-person past participle" (gives gave given). If the participle is the same as
#   the past, write two forms (finds found). For a noun: the plural. For an adjective: the comparative and the
#   superlative. If the line gives no forms, the tool makes the regular forms: plural -s, third person -s,
#   past -ed. "-" = the word has no other forms. The -ing form of a verb is not approved (STE rule for verbs).
#   "?" before a word: the author is NOT SURE that the official dictionary approves this word in this part of
#   speech. The tool counts these words. Examine them first when the official dictionary is available.
#   Text after "#" is a comment.
#
# Technical names and technical verbs are not in this list: see technical-names.json and the glossary
# (src/i18n/locales/en/90-glossary.json).
