About SANA

The Space Assigned Numbers Authority (SANA) is the registrar function for the protocol registries created under the Consultative Committee for Space Data Systems (CCSDS). SANA was created by the Yellow Book 313x0y02 under the SANA working group, as part of the Systems Engineering Area(SEA).

The SANA operations is provided by Cofomo under the secretariat of CCSDS.

The SANA's roles, responsibilities and procedures are defined in the CCSDS Yellow Book 313.0-Y-3. The SANA implements the CCSDS Yellow Book 313.1-Y-2 (CCSDS SANA Registry Management Policy).

To contact SANA, use info@sanaregistry.org.
