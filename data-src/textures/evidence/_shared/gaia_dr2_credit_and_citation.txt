Credit and citation instructions

If you have used Gaia data in your research, please use the following acknowledgement:

This work has made use of data from the European Space Agency (ESA) mission Gaia ( [https://www.cosmos.esa.int/gaia] https://www.cosmos.esa.int/gaia), processed by the Gaia Data Processing and Analysis Consortium (DPAC, [https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/gaia/dpac/consortium] https://www.cosmos.esa.int/web/gaia/dpac/consortium). Funding for the DPAC has been provided by national institutions, in particular the institutions participating in the Gaia Multilateral Agreement.

If you have used Gaia DR2 data in your research, please cite both the Gaia mission paper and the Gaia DR2 release paper:

•

Gaia Collaboration et al. ( [../../bib.html#bib178] 2016): Description of the Gaia mission (spacecraft, instruments, survey and measurement principles, and operations);

•

Gaia Collaboration et al. ( [../../bib.html#bib20] 2018b): Summary of the contents and survey properties.

In addition, please cite (some of) the following papers that describe the data release contents and DPAC data processing and validation in more detail, as appropriate:

•

Arenou et al. ( [../../bib.html#bib18] 2018): Catalogue validation;

•

Crowley et al. ( [../../bib.html#bib180] 2016): On-orbit performance of the Gaia CCDs;

•

•

•

Katz et al. ( [../../bib.html#bib7] 2018): Properties and validation of the radial velocities;
